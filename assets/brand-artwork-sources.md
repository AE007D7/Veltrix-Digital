# Original gift-card brand artwork

Vector marks retain their original outlines, with white monochrome variants for contrast on dark gift cards, a red Netflix mark, and the full-color Google Play mark.

14 marks were obtained from Simple Icons 11.15.0 (CC0): https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons
Google Play full-color artwork: https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos/blob/main/logos/google-play-icon.svg
Fortnite wordmark: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FortniteLogo.svg (Epic Games; public-domain text logo).
Veltrix header uses the store's existing assets/veltrix-original-logo.webp.

Third-party brand marks identify the corresponding products. They do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
