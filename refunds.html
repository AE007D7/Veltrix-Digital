Fair, clear support for digital orders

Refund eligibility for digital content depends on the product, whether it was delivered or used, the issue reported, the terms accepted before purchase, and applicable consumer-protection law.

← Back to storefront

Before payment

You can withdraw an enquiry at any time before payment. Submitting the enquiry form does not create a charge. For a purchase made through Stripe Checkout, use the contact form promptly if you need help with cancellation; applicable statutory rights continue to apply.

Before digital delivery

For an agreed purchase not yet delivered, contact us promptly if you wish to cancel. We assess cancellation under the agreed terms and applicable law.

After delivery or activation

Digital codes, activated memberships and other consumable digital goods may not qualify for a change-of-mind refund once delivered or used, where local law permits. This does not override mandatory consumer protections.

Invalid, unused or misdescribed products

If a digital product supplied by us is invalid, not as described, or fails due to a seller error, contact support. We will investigate with our supplier and provide the appropriate remedy under the agreed sale terms and applicable law, including replacement or refund where required.

How to ask for help

Use the contact page and include your name, order reference if supplied, date and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers or confidential access details.
