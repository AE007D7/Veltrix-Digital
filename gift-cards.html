What to check before choosing

PlayStation Store

PlayStation wallet vouchers are typically tied to the country of the PlayStation account. Confirm you have a US account before choosing a US code.

Steam Wallet

Wallet-code currency and account acceptance vary. Confirm the recipient account can redeem the selected denomination.

Xbox Gift Cards

Microsoft account currency and region affect gift-card redemption. Confirm that the account and gift-card country match.

Netflix Gift Cards

Netflix balance redemption depends on billing currency and account eligibility. Check the recipient account before ordering.

Amazon Gift Cards

Amazon gift cards are store-specific. A US Amazon.com gift card is not the same as a card for other country storefronts.

Nintendo eShop

Nintendo eShop codes are tied to the appropriate country/region and currency. Check the Nintendo Account region before purchase.

Apple Gift Cards

Apple account region and card currency must match the applicable store and redemption rules.

Before placing an order: These are US-region listings and currency references. Product stock, reseller permissions, card denominations, taxes and actual checkout availability must be checked. Card checkout is available only for approved products after Stripe activation.

Frequently asked questions

Do US gift cards work worldwide?

Not necessarily. Many digital gift cards require a matching account country, wallet currency or storefront. Buying a US code does not guarantee redemption in another country.

How do I choose the right denomination?

First select the platform and its correct account region. Then compare available face values and the final seller price, including any fees.

Can I pay by Visa or Mastercard?

Stripe Checkout is prepared for a limited set of eligible US-region gift cards. Card payment is not available until the live checkout configuration is activated.

Does Veltrix Digital own the game and streaming brands?

No. Veltrix Digital is an independent storefront. Brand names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
