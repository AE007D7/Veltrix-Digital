Gift card questions

What is the difference between face value and selling price?

The face value is the credit the recipient redeems. The selling price is the USD amount you pay to Veltrix, displayed before checkout.

Can a gift card be used in any country?

Many cards are region locked. Match the card's country, currency and platform to the recipient's account before buying.

Does buying a card require the recipient’s password?

Digital codes and gift links are redeemed by the recipient on the official service. Checkout does not collect service passwords.

Brand names identify compatible services. Veltrix is independent of these brands. See our delivery guide, refund policy and support page.
