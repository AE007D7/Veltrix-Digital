Gift card questions

What is the difference between face value and selling price?

Face value is the credit added to the service account. Selling price is the amount charged by Veltrix, shown in USD on each product page.

Can I buy a gift without the recipient's password?

These gift card and gift-link options are redeemed by the recipient on their own account. Veltrix checkout does not collect service passwords.

Which gift card should I choose?

Choose a platform the recipient already uses: Roblox or PlayStation for gaming, Battle.net for Blizzard titles, Twitch for supporting streamers, or Discord Nitro for a digital subscription. Check the exact regional requirements on the product page.
