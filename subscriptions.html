Subscription questions

Does checkout start a recurring subscription?

No. Veltrix charges a one-time payment for the selected plan or code. No automatic renewal is started by our checkout.

Can every plan be activated in every country?

No. Plan availability and account eligibility differ. Review the activation region and notes on the product page.

Is activation instant?

Delivery and activation are processed after payment confirmation. Instant fulfilment is not guaranteed. Contact support before paying if you need a specific delivery time.
