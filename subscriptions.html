What to check before choosing

Gaming time subscriptions

Availability of game-time codes and account-compatible plans depends on the title, country, platform, and supplier terms. Confirm the correct game and region before ordering.

Music subscriptions

Music service plans may require local billing methods, student or family eligibility, and country-specific entitlements. Shared logins are not a substitute for an authorized subscription.

AI software subscriptions

AI subscriptions are subject to the official provider's account, billing, and resale terms. Veltrix does not claim authorized resale of ChatGPT or Gemini subscriptions without appropriate permission.

Internet TV subscriptions

TV streaming may only be offered when the content distributor has the necessary licenses and region rights. Unsupported or unauthorized streams are not offered.

Delivery and security

Do not provide account passwords or two-factor authentication codes in a product enquiry. Review activation method, renewal terms, product region, and cancellation rules before any payment.

Before placing an order: Subscription listings are enquiries only unless authorized resale and a supported checkout method are verified. We do not advertise guaranteed discounts, shared accounts, or subscriptions that breach provider terms.

Frequently asked questions

Can subscription plans work in any country?

No. Billing country, activation region, account type and local availability differ across platforms. Verify the correct terms for the intended recipient.

Are accounts automatically created or delivered?

No automatic account fulfilment is promised. Activation and delivery methods must be verified for each product before any sale is agreed.

Can I purchase ChatGPT or Gemini access through this website?

Veltrix Digital does not currently offer verified authorization to resell these plans. Such listings are not available for checkout.

Are advertised discounts guaranteed?

No. Final prices, reseller permissions, entitlements and availability must be verified before a genuine offer can be made.
