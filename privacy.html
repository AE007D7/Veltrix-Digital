VELTRIX DIGITAL / PRIVACY POLICY

We respect your information

This page explains how Veltrix Digital handles information on the website and through the product request form. Effective 12 October 2026.

← Back to storefront

Information you provide

If you send an enquiry, we collect your name, email, optional country, requested product and any message. If you buy through secure checkout, it is hosted by Stripe, which processes payment-card and checkout details. Veltrix may receive customer, order and transaction details needed to handle the purchase. Do not send passwords or card numbers through the enquiry form.

How it is used

We use information you provide to respond to enquiries, process and fulfill purchases, provide support, prevent abuse and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell customer contact lists.

Storage and service providers

Your shopping selection is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Enquiry details are transmitted securely to the hosting environment and an email provider configured by the operator. Stripe processes checkout and payment data for purchases. These providers may process data in different jurisdictions.

Third-party resources

We use Google Fonts for typography. Viewing this site may result in Google receiving technical information such as your IP address. Product links and third-party services have separate privacy policies. We do not deploy advertising or analytics tracking scripts in this version.

Retention and your rights

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of enquiry information where legally applicable. We keep information only for as long as needed for the purpose of the request, dispute handling, and applicable recordkeeping duties. Your local cart can be cleared in the cart interface or your browser storage settings.

Contact for privacy requests

Use our contact page and select a privacy-related reason. The business contact email must be provided by the operator before publicly opening enquiries.
